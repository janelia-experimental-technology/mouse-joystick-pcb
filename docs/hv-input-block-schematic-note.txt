--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TPS1HTC100-Q1 SMART HIGH-SIDE SWITCH - PER-BOARD RESISTOR SELECTION

R_ILIM sets the current limit. R_SNS sets the ADC sense scaling. They do
independent jobs, but R_SNS has a ceiling that depends on I_CL - see below.

PREREQUISITES, or neither resistor does anything:
  DIAG_EN must be driven HIGH. Left floating (internal pulldown) the analog
    sense, open-load and short-to-battery diagnostics are all disabled.
  ILIM must NEVER float. Floating selects the internal default limit AND is
    "considered a fault state for the device" (datasheet Table 4-2).

--- R_ILIM (single resistor to GND) -> CURRENT LIMIT -------------------------
  I_CL = K_CL / R_ILIM        Transient peak allowance: I_CL_LINPK = 1.5 x I_CL

  R_ILIM        I_CL min    typ     max     source
  49.9k / 50k    0.73 A    0.92 A   1.11 A  datasheet
  40k            0.9  A    1.2  A   1.4  A  computed
  25k            1.5  A    1.96 A   2.3  A  datasheet
  20k            1.9  A    2.5  A   2.9  A  computed
  16.7k          2.3  A    3.0  A   3.5  A  datasheet
  10k            4.0  A    5.3  A   6.3  A  datasheet
  open or GND    6.1  A    7.3  A   8.6  A  datasheet - do not use

  Adjustable range is 1 A to 5 A, or 7.3 A with ILIM at GND. Pick a limit that
  clears the board's worst-case load AND passes the inrush into the downstream
  bulk capacitance, since charging above ~10 uF is current-limited.

--- R_SNS (single resistor to GND) -> SENSE SCALING -------------------------
  V_SNS = (I_OUT / 800) * R_SNS

  TWO CEILINGS, BOTH MUST HOLD:

  1. FAULT LEVEL - does NOT depend on I_CL.
     In fault the pin sources I_SNSFLT = 5.2-6.4 mA into R_SNS.
       R_SNS <= V_ADC_MAX / 6.4 mA
     3.3 V ADC -> 515 ohm absolute; use <= 470 ohm for margin.
     Reducing R_SNS is the only fix. The alternative clamp level V_SNSFH is
     3.3-4.4 V at DIAG_EN = 3.3 V and 4.5-5.8 V at DIAG_EN = 5 V, both of which
     exceed a 3.3 V ADC input.

  2. CURRENT RANGE - scales with I_CL.
       R_SNS <= V_ADC_MAX * 800 / (1.5 * I_CL)
     3.3 V ADC -> R_SNS <= 1760 / I_CL(A) ohm

  CONSEQUENCE: ceiling 1 binds up to I_CL = 3.4 A, so for any limit at or below
  that, R_SNS DOES NOT NEED TO CHANGE when R_ILIM changes. Above 3.4 A, scale
  R_SNS down per ceiling 2.

  I_CL      R_SNS max, 3.3 V ADC     which ceiling binds
  1 A         515 ohm                fault level
  2 A         515 ohm                fault level
  3 A         515 ohm                fault level
  3.4 A       515 ohm                crossover
  5 A         350 ohm                current range
  7.3 A       240 ohm                current range

  249 ohm satisfies both ceilings across the part's entire adjustable range and
  is a safe single value for every board:
    0.311 V/A, full scale 10.6 A, fault level 1.29-1.59 V.
  At I_CL = 7.3 A a 1.5x transient peak reads 3.41 V - the reading saturates,
  nothing is damaged.

  Larger R_SNS buys ADC amplitude, not accuracy. I_SNS accuracy is +-5 to 6%
  above ~0.5 A but +-25 to 30% below 60 mA regardless of R_SNS, so this is a
  coarse supply monitor by construction.

  For a 5 V ADC, ceiling 1 becomes 781 ohm and ceiling 2 becomes 2670/I_CL.

--- ADC INPUT ---------------------------------------------------------------
  RP2040 absolute maximum on an ADC pin is VDD + 0.3 V = 3.6 V. Size both
  ceilings against 3.0-3.3 V, not 3.6 V.
  SNS also reports a fault by driving high, so the fault level - not the
  maximum current - is usually what sets R_SNS.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Worked example, mouse-joystick-pcb: one 0.67 A/phase stepper on a 36 V rail draws
roughly 0.2-0.3 A while holding. R_ILIM = 40k gives I_CL ~1.2 A, about 4x
headroom. That is below the 3.4 A crossover, so R_SNS is chosen purely by the
fault ceiling: 249 ohm, the largest stocked value comfortably under 470 ohm.

Stocked combinations at the time of writing, for the values not available as a
single resistor:
  25k   = 2 x 49.9k in parallel   (OW-PASS-RC0402FR-0749K9L)
  20k   = 2 x 40k in parallel     (OW-PASS-SCR0603F40K)
  249R  = single                  (OW-PASS-CRCW0402249RFKED)
Re-check stock rather than trusting this list; the two tables above do not
depend on it.
